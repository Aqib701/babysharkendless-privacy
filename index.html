This Privacy Policy explains how Baby Shark Endless (“the app”, “the game”), published by AllOut Games (“we”, “us”, “our”), accesses, collects, uses and shares information. In short: we do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no chat or social features. Your game progress is saved on your device. The game does not have permission to access the internet.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data. The app does not access, collect or ask for:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • account details or login credentials (the game has no accounts or sign-in);
  • precise or approximate location;
  • photos, videos, audio, microphone or camera data;
  • contacts, calendar, messages or files;
  • advertising IDs, device identifiers (such as IMEI or Android ID) or other persistent identifiers;
  • health, financial or payment information;
  • browsing history, app-usage analytics or crash reports sent from within the app.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your progress between sessions, the game saves the following on your device, in the app’s own storage:

  • your best time (high score);
  • the number of gems you have;
  • which sharks you have unlocked and which one is selected;
  • your sound setting;
  • your daily reward streak and the day you last claimed a reward (taken from your device’s date).

Gems are earned by playing and from daily rewards. They are used only inside the game and cannot be bought with real money.

The game does not send this information to us or to anyone else. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app’s saved data in your own device backup (for example, in your Google account) so it can be restored later. Google runs that backup under Google’s Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can manage backups in your device settings.

3. How we use information

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use it for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose. The saved game data described in section 2 is used only to run the game on your device.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer user data to any third party. The game contains no advertising networks, analytics services or social-media SDKs, and no other third-party service that collects user data.

The following happen outside the game and are handled by other companies:

  • Links that open in your browser or another app. The “More Games” button opens our developer page on Google Play, and the “Privacy Policy” button opens this page. What you do there is covered by that service’s own privacy policy.
  • Google Play. If you get the game through Google Play, Google handles the download, installation and any store reports (such as Android vitals, according to your device settings) under Google’s Privacy Policy. Reports Google shows developers are not used by us to identify you.
  • This web page. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you open it, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not receive that information.

5. Permissions

The game does not request any Android permissions. It has no access to the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, messages, calendar or shared storage. The game plays fully offline.

6. Data security

We do not receive personal data and we run no servers for the game. Data saved by the game is kept in the app’s private storage area, which Android protects from other apps. We recommend keeping your device up to date and protected with a screen lock.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any user data, because we do not receive any. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. To delete it, clear the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Baby Shark Endless → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the game. A copy kept in your Android device backup may be restored when you reinstall; you can remove it through your device’s or Google account’s backup settings. The game has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

8. Children’s privacy

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). It shows no ads, has no purchases with real money and offers no way for players to communicate or share information. The “More Games” and “Privacy Policy” buttons leave the game, so we encourage parents and guardians to supervise young children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or to object to its sale or sharing. Since the game collects no personal data, we hold none from your play. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if we add ads or any feature that collects data), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and update the game’s Google Play “Data safety” information before releasing the change.
